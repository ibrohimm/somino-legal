What Somino is

Somino is a personal finance tool: it records what you enter, plans upcoming bills and shows estimates such as "You can spend today". It is not a bank, does not hold or move your money, and is not financial, tax or legal advice. Numbers depend on what you enter and on exchange rates that may be delayed. Check important decisions yourself.

  • In a family, you decide which wallets are shared. Only invite people you trust; members can see and edit what is shared.
