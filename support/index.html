Somino – Support

We usually reply within two working days. Write to ibrohim625m@gmail.com, or use Profile → Help → Contact support in the app (it adds your app version for us).

Common questions

  • My entries are not backed up. Sign in with Google or Apple in Profile. The badge turns green when everything is safe; tap "Back up" to sync right away.
  • New phone. Install Somino and sign in with the same account; your entries come back automatically.
  • Family. One person creates the family (Somino Pro) and shares the invite code; others join for free and choose which wallets to share.
  • Restore Somino Pro. Open the Somino Pro screen and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with.
  • Cancel a subscription. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Delete my account. Profile → your account → Delete account. This removes all synced data from our server.
