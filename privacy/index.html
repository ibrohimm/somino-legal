Somino – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Somino (formerly Money Manager; "we", "the app") helps you track spending, plan bills before payday and manage money across two homes. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Your data stays on your phone unless you sign in

You can use Somino without an account. Everything you enter is stored only in the app's database on your device. Nothing is uploaded until you choose to sign in with Google or Apple to back up and sync.

If you sign in: what is synced, and why

When you sign in, your entries are backed up to our server so you can restore them on a new phone, use several devices, and share with your family. We sync:

  • Your account: the email address and name that Google or Apple share with us (with Apple you can hide your email; we then see a private relay address). You can change the name in the app.
  • Your money records: expenses and income (amount, currency, date and time zone, the name you typed, category, wallet), categories and subcategories, wallets and their starting balances, budgets, repeating payments, bills, installments and debts, transfers, and money sent home (amount, fee, amount received, and the person it was for).
  • People you add: the names, relation (family, friend, other) and currency of people you send money to or share debts with. They are only labels for your records; we never contact them.
  • Family (optional): if you create or join a family, its name, the members' display names, invite codes, and the wallets, entries and budgets you choose to share. Members of your family can see what is shared with the family; entries in your private wallets are never shared.

Receipt photos and receipt QR scans stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, backup and sync). Privacy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics). Privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscriptions). Privacy
  • Google and Apple (Sign in with Google, Sign in with Apple, App Store and Google Play payments). Google · Apple
  • Open Exchange Rates provides currency rates to our server. The app downloads the rate table; none of your data is sent to them.

Permissions

  • Camera. Only to scan the QR code on a fiscal receipt. The image is processed on your device and not saved. Opening a scanned receipt shows it on the tax authority's website (ofd.soliq.uz).
  • Notifications. For bill reminders, the evening "close the day" reminder and the weekly summary. They are scheduled on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID. Optional, for App Lock. Biometric data never leaves your device's secure hardware.

You can turn any permission off in your phone's settings at any time.

Your choices, keeping and deleting data

  • Export all your entries anytime to CSV or Excel (Profile → Export).
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → your account → Delete account). This permanently removes your account and all synced data from our server. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app.
  • Leave a family at any time; its shared entries are then removed from your phone.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase under its standard retention (up to 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics).

To ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, contact us below.

Children

Somino is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
